# Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** 24.11.2026

These guidelines describe how to use the Bot's community and group tools fairly. They are a practical companion to the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) and [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md); if anything conflicts, the Terms govern. The operator and group admins may moderate accordingly.

## 1. Be decent

* No harassment, threats, defamation, stalking, hate, sexual content involving minors, or illegal content. CSAM is prohibited absolutely.
* No spam, phishing, malware, scam links, unsolicited bulk messages, or mass mentions.
* Do not impersonate staff, group admins or the operator, and do not file false reports or abuse tickets.
* Do not evade warnings, mutes or bans by using another number or account.

## 2. Group roles and admin approvals

* Group admins decide who is promoted or demoted. `#pr` (self-promotion) and `#dm` (self-demotion) are requests, not switches: they take effect only after a group admin replies to the Bot's card with `#pr approve` / `#dm approve` (or `reject`). The Bot must itself be a group admin.
* Do not pressure, bribe or impersonate an admin to obtain an approval. A non-admin who tries to approve receives a warning and the request stays pending until a real admin decides.
* One open self-role request per person and type. Use `#pr cancel` / `#dm cancel` if you change your mind; pending requests expire after 24 hours.
* `#apr` / `#adm` are sudo-only and bypass group approval by design. They are not a workaround for users without that rank.
* Accept the outcome. Role decisions can be revisited or reversed by group admins or the operator.

## 3. Asking the Bot to join a group

* `#request <chat.whatsapp.com/...>` is for groups you are legitimately allowed to invite the Bot into. Use a current invite link and act on behalf of the members.
* Do not submit fake, expired or revoked links, do not mass-request, and do not link groups you have no right to add the Bot to. Only one request per person may be open at a time; it expires after 24 hours.
* A rejection is final for that request and the decision is sent to you by DM. Wait before asking again and fix the reason for the rejection first.
* Team+ in the receiver group decides with `#rq approve` / `#rq reject` on the Bot's card. Do not ping or pressure them, and do not approve a request you have not checked.
* The join link is posted in the receiver group with the request and again after approval so Team+ can enter. Do not reshare it outside the team, and never reuse a link from a rejected request.

## 4. Setup tokens

* After an approval the Bot joins the target group and issues a one-time `#setupaccept <token>` token, valid for 24 hours. Only Team+ in that group should run it.
* Do not share the token outside the group. It enables welcome, goodbye, antilink and antispam there and is consumed on first use.
* If nobody accepts in time, ask for a fresh request.

## 5. Reporting problems

* Use `#report <reason>` for abuse and `#ticket` / `#support` for help. Provide truthful details; false reports violate the Terms.
* Group admins remain responsible for the moderation tools they enable in their own groups.
